Lessons & coaching.

Building voices through classical technique applied to any genre, acting technique, and confidence-building.

Julia works with singers of all ages and experience levels, from young students discovering their voices for the first time to high school and college singers preparing for auditions, performances, and conservatory programs. She also works with advanced vocalists seeking to refine technique, expand repertoire, and build confidence across classical and contemporary styles.

Lessons are grounded in classical bel canto technique and adapted to each student's individual goals, including opera repertoire, musical theater auditions, legit and contemporary belt technique, jazz and vocal improvisation, pop and singer-songwriter styles, rock performance, or commercial contemporary music. Emphasis is placed on healthy vocal production, breath support, musical expression, stylistic versatility, performance confidence, and developing a sustainable technique across multiple genres.

  • Classical technique rooted in bel canto
  • Genre coaching across MT, pop, and jazz
  • Audition & conservatory preparation
  • Repertoire selection and programming
