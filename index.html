Julia Kempf

Vocal artistry & coaching.

Classically trained and stylistically versatile, Julia performs across opera, musical theater, jazz, pop, and studio recording work. Her teaching combines healthy bel canto vocal technique with acting-based interpretation, stylistic flexibility, and confidence-building, helping singers of all ages and levels develop expressive, sustainable, and authentic voices across genres.
